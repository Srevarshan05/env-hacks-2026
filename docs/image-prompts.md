## Final prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a single ultra-tall continuous photographic panorama used as the background of five vertically scrolling full-screen website slides. Create an ultra-tall PORTRAIT image with a 1:3 width-to-height aspect ratio, ideally 1024 x 3072 pixels. This is one completely connected natural scene, not a collage, not a grid, no borders, no text. The camera travels vertically down the same lush Indian forest waterfall into the river. Composition from TOP to BOTTOM: top 20 percent is a spacious pale blue morning sky with soft white clouds, very subtle treetop edges, atmospheric daylight; next 20 percent shows the upper lip of a beautiful waterfall in a steep forest gorge, with water beginning to fall; next 20 percent follows that same falling stream cascading past realistic mossy rock walls; next 20 percent reaches the clear jade river surface with waterfall spray and small concentric ripples; bottom 20 percent is a believable underwater view through exceptionally clear freshwater, showing a large naturally textured stone covered in short green algae and tiny sparkling oxygen bubbles rising from the algae. Keep the waterfall and algae-covered rock in the right half of the image. Leave the left half calmer and less detailed for overlaid website copy. Exact alignment between all zones, the same location and same water stream from top to bottom. Soft daylight beams reach into the water. Medium dark forest green, muted jade water, neutral moss colors; the sky is light. Make this feel like beautifully photographed real nature, refined and clean, realistic natural materials and tiny details, no vector shapes, no painting, no stylized CG scenery. Do not include a purifier device, people, buildings, writing, logos, labels, typography, watermarks, or any artificial UI. All five stages must be visible in one uninterrupted image.
